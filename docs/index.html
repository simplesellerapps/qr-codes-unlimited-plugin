5. Answer product questions from reviewed help

How do I show my own domain when a QR code is scanned?

Tools: get_product_help, get_account, get_product_help

Connect a custom subdomain, such as `qr.example.com`, to brand your QR code’s scan link. Your Growth plan supports this, and no custom domains are currently connected.

1. Choose an unused subdomain of a domain you own.
2. At your DNS provider, add a record:
   - **Type:** CNAME
   - **Name/Host:** `qr`
   - **Target/Value:** `custom.o2o.to`
   - **TTL:** Default (Cloudflare: use **DNS only**).
3. Open [Links → Domains](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/qr/my/domains), enter `qr.example.com` without `https://` or a path, run the check, and save.
4. Select the domain in your QR code’s settings, then download and test the new QR image.

This brands the scan link; each code’s final destination is configured separately. Existing printed codes retain their original link, so keep that domain working and reprint to display your new domain.

[Full setup guide](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/guides/custom-qr-code-domain)
