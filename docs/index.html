How do I show my own domain when a QR code is scanned?

Tools: get_product_help, get_account, get_product_help

Connect a custom subdomain, such as `qr.example.com`, to brand your QR code’s scan link. Your Growth plan supports this, and no custom domains are currently connected.

1. Choose an unused subdomain of a domain you own.
2. At your DNS provider, add a record:
   - **Type:** CNAME
   - **Name/Host:** `qr`
   - **Target/Value:** `custom.o2o.to`
   - **TTL:** Default (Cloudflare: use **DNS only**).
3. Open [Links → Domains](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/qr/my/domains), enter `qr.example.com` without `https://` or a path, run the check, and save.
4. Select the domain in your QR code’s settings, then download and test the new QR image.

This brands the scan link; each code’s final destination is configured separately. Existing printed codes retain their original link, so keep that domain working and reprint to display your new domain.

[Full setup guide](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/guides/custom-qr-code-domain)

5. Answer product questions from reviewed help

How do I show my own domain when a QR code is scanned?

Tools: get_product_help, get_account

To show your own domain, connect a **custom subdomain** such as `qr.yourbrand.com`. Your QR codes then encode `qr.yourbrand.com/...` instead of the default `qrunlmtd.com/...`.

**Your account:** You're on the Growth plan, which includes custom domains. You don't have any custom domains set up yet, so your codes currently use `qrunlmtd.com`.

## Steps

1. **Pick an unused subdomain** of a domain you own, like `qr.yourbrand.com`. Use a subdomain, not the main domain (`yourbrand.com`) or `www`.

2. **Add a CNAME record** wherever your domain's DNS is managed (your domain registrar or DNS host):
   - **Type:** CNAME
   - **Name/Host:** `qr`
   - **Target/Value:** `custom.o2o.to` (no `https://` and no path)
   - **TTL:** default

   Don't change the records for your website or email.
   - **Cloudflare:** set the record to **DNS only** (gray cloud) and TTL to Auto.
   - **GoDaddy / Shopify:** see the guides linked below.

3. **Add the domain in QR Codes Unlimited:** go to **Links › Domains**, enter the full subdomain and save. The domain only becomes active once its DNS check passes, and DNS changes can take a while to spread. I can also add it and run the check for you.

4. **Assign it to your codes.** If you have more than one domain, you choose the domain in each code's settings. I can switch your existing 4 codes to the new domain once it's active.

5. **Download and print new QR images, then test one.** Scan it to confirm it shows your domain and goes to the right page.

## Things to know
- **The domain only changes the scan link.** Where each code sends people is still set on the code itself.
- **Codes you've already printed keep the old link,** because the link is part of the printed image. They keep working through `qrunlmtd.com`. If you ever use a custom domain and later remove it, codes printed with it will stop working.

**Guides:** [Custom domain setup](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/guides/custom-qr-code-domain) · [Cloudflare](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/guides/custom-qr-code-domain/cloudflare) · [GoDaddy](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/guides/custom-qr-code-domain/godaddy) · [Shopify](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/guides/custom-qr-code-domain/shopify) · [Your domain settings](https://qrcodesunlimited.com/qr/my/domains)

I haven't changed anything in your account. Send me the subdomain you'd like to use and I'll add it and give you the exact CNAME record to create.
